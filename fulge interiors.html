About FULGE INTERIORS

Born and raised in Mbarara, Uganda, FULGE INTERIORS blends contemporary design with the warmth of East African craftsmanship. We believe interiors tell your story — sophistication, comfort, and authentic character.

Our team of interior architects and artisans collaborates closely with local woodworkers, weavers, and metal artists to create furniture that speaks elegance. Whether it's a cozy residential project or a flagship commercial interior, we bring Fulge’s signature touch to every corner of Western Uganda and beyond.

Since 2018, we’ve served over 300 happy clients across Mbarara, Kampala, and the region. We’re driven by quality, integrity, and timeless design.
